When she asks or pushes back

Objections

Any"I don't have time for another call."

I understand that you are busy, because I am busy too. Maybe you are saying that because you are not sure the call will give you enough value to take time away from your schedule.

I would not ask you to invest your time unless I knew it would help you get clear on your goals, so you can make better decisions in the days and weeks ahead about HER PROBLEM.

Any"I already tried Facebook ads."

One PMU artist can do microblading and another artist can do the same microblading, and they will get totally different results. It is the same with marketing. Two marketers can run the same type of campaign on Facebook and get totally different results, because it is an art.

And it is never only about the Facebook ads. There are other things we do that come together to make those leads excited and give them value.

Any"Do you do Facebook ads?"

Yes, we run Facebook and Instagram ads, but it is not just about running ads. What makes the difference is how you run them. We also have a full qualification system that nurtures and warms up the leads. To build a successful PMU business, ads are just one piece of the puzzle. You need the whole system working together. Does that make sense?

Any"How does it work?"

NoteGive enough to satisfy her, not every detail.

That's a great question. To be a successful permanent makeup artist, three things must happen. One, generate the opportunities: we bring the potential clients in through social media. Two, qualify them and get them excited about the service. Three, turn them into bookings. We help you with all three.

Any"Do you do lips and eyeliner, or other PMU services?"

We can do eyeliner and lips, but we mainly focus on eyebrows. Eyebrows are the most wanted service right now, so we can bring in more people for eyebrows at a lower cost than if we put eyeliner or lips up front.

You will also make more money this way. After a client does her eyebrows with you and she is happy and excited, you can offer her eyeliner or lips, and she is much more likely to say yes. If we bring her in for lips or eyeliner first, she may not need the eyebrows.

Any"Do you recommend that I require a deposit?"

Absolutely! Your time is valuable, and a deposit is super important. We will show you how to build a personal connection and trust with your clients, so they are happy to pay the deposit.

Any"Will you discount my services?"

One thing we will never do is discount the services of our PMU artists. Our goal is to make our artists as profitable as we can.

People pay for value. As long as they see the value in what you offer, they pay the full amount. If they don't see it, they ask for a discount. We will show you how to increase the value of your services so you can charge more.

Any"How many artists do you work with?"

We have worked with more than 400 PMU artists across the country, over more than 8 years. In the past we served many different types of businesses. At some point we decided to let go of all the others and focus 100% on PMU.

I believe it is more professional, both for us and for the artists. We know all the problems and we have all the solutions. Much better for everyone.

Any"How can you advertise for me if my Facebook account is restricted?"

That is very common, and it depends on the situation. We have worked with more than 400 PMU artists, so we know exactly how to fix it.

We can appeal, we can create a new Facebook business account, or in the worst case we can collect the ad spend and pay Facebook on your behalf.
